Project 01

Integrating Simulations and Observations

A Foundation Model for Estimating the Aerosol Mixing State Index

[cite_start]Accurately predicting aerosol mixing states in real-world environments is crucial for understanding their impacts on climate change and human health[cite: 19]. [cite_start]However, observational data inherently exhibit spatiotemporal gaps[cite: 20].

In this study, we frame the emulation of aerosol simulation as a general task. [cite_start]We developed a foundation model pretrained on particle-resolved simulations (PartMC-MOSAIC) and fine-tuned it using observational data from the MEGAPOLI field campaign[cite: 38].
